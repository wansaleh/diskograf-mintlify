The community builds Diskograf, and its data should be useful beyond the site. This page covers what you can reuse, how to credit us, and what the licence excludes.

## What You Can Reuse

Factual data is open for reuse. This covers song titles, release years, credits, artist and label names, and genres. Get it through the [Public API](/developers/public-api) or from catalogue pages. You may:

- Query the API from your own applications, sites, or research tools.
- Cache and store reasonable amounts of data to power your project.
- Cite Diskograf as a source in articles, papers, videos, and reference works.
- Build derivative datasets and visualizations that clearly credit Diskograf.

## Attribution

When you show or share meaningful amounts of data, credit Diskograf and link to the relevant page.

A minimum acceptable form:

```text
Source: Diskograf (https://diskograf.com)
```

For datasets or research, also link the specific artist, song, or label pages you used.

## What Is Not Covered

Diskograf does not own some content, so it cannot license it:

- **Cover art, artist photos, and label logos** belong to their rights holders. Diskograf shows them under fair use for reference only. Do not scrape or redistribute them.
- **YouTube videos and embedded media** play under YouTube's own terms.
- **Song lyrics**, if shown, belong to their authors and publishers.
- **User content**, such as profile text or biographies, belongs to the contributor.

## What You Cannot Do

- Do not imply endorsement or an official partnership.
- Do not scrape the site instead of using the API. Use `api.diskograf.com`.
- Do not send sustained traffic that slows the API for others. See the rate limits on the [Public API](/developers/public-api) page.
- Do not republish artist or label images as part of a Diskograf-licensed dataset.

## Commercial Use

Small commercial use with credit is generally welcome. Planning something larger, such as a product built mainly on Diskograf data? Contact us first through the [Contact](/support/contact) page.
