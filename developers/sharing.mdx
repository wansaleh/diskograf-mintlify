Every song, artist, genre, and label has a stable URL you can share. Most links show a preview card with an image, title, and description on social and chat apps.

## Canonical URLs

URLs follow simple patterns. Type them yourself or copy them from the address bar.

| Resource | Pattern | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Song | `/song/:songId` | `https://diskograf.com/song/12345` |
| Unclaimed artist | `/artist/:artistId` | `https://diskograf.com/artist/589` |
| Linked artist profile | `/:username` | `https://diskograf.com/norlisa` |
| Label | `/label/:labelId` | `https://diskograf.com/label/42` |
| Genre | `/genre/:slug` | `https://diskograf.com/genre/pop` |
| User profile | `/:username` | `https://diskograf.com/norlisa` |

These URLs are stable. IDs never change, and old slugs redirect. A linked artist profile uses the member's username path. The old `/artist/...` path redirects there.

## Social Preview Cards

Song pages have a generated Open Graph preview card. Paste a song link into WhatsApp, X, Facebook, LinkedIn, Bluesky, Discord, or similar apps. The app shows the card instead of a plain link.

The card shows the title, main artist, release year, and cover art when available. Cards are built on demand and cached at the edge. The first request may be a bit slower.

Artist and label pages also have standard Open Graph data: a title, description, and image.

## Sharing From the Site

Use your browser's share menu or copy the address on any catalogue page. The copied URL is already the canonical one.

## Tips

- **Link to filtered views.** Browse filters are stored in the URL. A shared link opens the same filtered view.
- **Refresh a stale preview.** Most platforms have a debugger to fetch a fresh card, such as Facebook's Sharing Debugger.
- **Link to detail pages in articles.** Link to the song, artist, or label page, not search results. Detail pages are the most stable.

<Note>
  Only the public catalogue gets preview cards. Private pages, drafts, and reports do not.
</Note>
