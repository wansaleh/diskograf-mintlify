This Privacy Policy explains how Diskograf Ent. collects, uses, and shares your information on [https://diskograf.com](https://diskograf.com). By using the service, you acknowledge these practices. **Last updated: July 18, 2026.**

## Information We Collect

We collect these categories of information:

**Personal data**: what you give us when you register or use the service:
- Email address
- First name and last name
- Profile photo (if you upload one)

**Usage data**: collected automatically when you use Diskograf:
- IP address
- Browser type and version
- Pages you visit and time spent on them
- Date and time of visits
- Device identifiers

**Google sign-in data**: if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email, and related Google account activity for sign-in. Google's privacy policy also covers this data.

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- **Provide the service**: run Diskograf and its features
- **Manage your account**: create, secure, and administer it
- **Contact you**: send service notices, updates, and replies by email or push notification
- **Check artist identity claims**: assess claims by users who say they are artists, and investigate suspected impersonation
- **Improve the service**: understand how people use Diskograf
- **Meet legal duties**: comply with applicable law and lawful requests

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

## Cookies and Tracking

Diskograf uses cookies and web beacons to run the service and personalise your experience:

- **Essential / Session cookies**: needed for the service to work. They start with your session and expire when you close your browser. Without them, core features break.
- **Cookie preference cookies** (persistent): record that you accepted our cookie policy, so we do not ask again. They stay on your device across sessions.
- **Functionality cookies** (persistent): remember preferences such as display settings. They stay on your device across sessions.

You can manage cookies in your browser. Blocking essential cookies may break some features.

## Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it in these cases:

- **Service providers**: vendors that help us run Diskograf, such as hosting and analytics. Contracts require them to protect your data and use it only as we direct.
- **Partners and affiliates**: trusted partners and affiliates, to run or improve the service.
- **Other users**: public content, such as your profile, loved songs, and activity, is visible to others.
- **Business transfers**: in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your data may transfer. We will post a prominent notice on the site.
- **Legal reasons**: when law, a court order, or a government authority requires it. Also when needed to protect the rights, property, or safety of Diskograf, its users, or the public.

## Data Retention

We keep personal data as long as this policy's purposes need it, or as the law requires. We usually keep usage data for shorter periods, because it serves short-term analytics. When we no longer need data, we delete or anonymise it.
