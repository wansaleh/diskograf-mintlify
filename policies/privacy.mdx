This Privacy Policy describes how Diskograf Ent. collects, uses, and shares your information when you use Diskograf at [https://diskograf.com](https://diskograf.com). By using the service, you acknowledge the practices described here. **Last updated: July 18, 2026.**

## Information We Collect

We collect information in the following categories:

**Personal data** — provided by you when you register or use the service:
- Email address
- First name and last name
- Profile photo (if you upload one)

**Usage data** — collected automatically when you access Diskograf:
- IP address
- Browser type and version
- Pages you visit and time spent on them
- Date and time of visits
- Device identifiers

**Google sign-in data** — if you choose to sign in using Google, we receive your name, email address, and associated activity from your Google account as part of the authentication process. This data is governed by Google's own privacy policy in addition to ours.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

- **Providing and maintaining the service**: to operate Diskograf and deliver the features you use
- **Managing your account**: to create, secure, and administer your account
- **Contacting you**: to send important service notices, updates, and responses to your enquiries via email or push notification
- **Verifying artist identity claims**: to assess and validate claims made by users representing themselves as artists, and to investigate suspected impersonation
- **Service improvement and analytics**: to understand how users engage with Diskograf and improve the experience over time
- **Legal compliance**: to meet our obligations under applicable law or respond to lawful requests

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

## Cookies and Tracking

Diskograf uses browser cookies and web beacons to keep the service running and to personalise your experience. Here is how each type of cookie is used:

- **Essential / Session cookies**: these cookies are necessary for the service to function. They are created at the start of your session and expire when you close your browser. Without them, core features of Diskograf will not work correctly.
- **Cookie preference cookies** (persistent): these cookies record your acceptance of our cookie policy so that you are not asked to consent again on every visit. They remain on your device across sessions.
- **Functionality cookies** (persistent): these cookies remember your preferences — such as display settings — so your experience stays consistent across visits. They remain on your device across sessions.

You can manage cookie settings in your browser. Disabling essential cookies may affect your ability to use certain features of the service.

## Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. However, we may share your information in the following circumstances:

- **Service providers**: we share data with third-party vendors and service providers who help us operate and maintain Diskograf (for example, hosting and analytics providers). These parties are contractually required to protect your data and use it only as directed.
- **Business partners and affiliates**: we may share data with trusted partners and affiliated entities in connection with operating or improving the service.
- **Other users**: content you post in public areas of Diskograf — including your profile, loved songs list, and activity — is visible to other users of the platform.
- **Business transfers**: if Diskograf Ent. is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any such change via a prominent notice on the site.
- **Law enforcement and legal obligations**: we may disclose your information where required by law, court order, or government authority, or where we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Diskograf, its users, or the public.
