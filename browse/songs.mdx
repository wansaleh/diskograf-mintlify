Each song on Diskograf is a full record of who made it. It covers everyone from the composer to the producer.

## The Song Catalogue

All songs are listed at [diskograf.com/songs](https://diskograf.com/songs). There you can:

- **Browse recent additions**: see newly added and updated songs.
- **Search**: type a title or phrase. Search works from any page.

## Song Pages

Each song has its own page at `diskograf.com/song/{id}-{slug}`. For example: [diskograf.com/song/20926-tersakiti](https://diskograf.com/song/20926-tersakiti).

A song page shows:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The song's canonical title, including non-Latin scripts where applicable |
| **Release Year** | The year the song was released |
| **Performing Artist(s)** | The artist or artists who recorded the song |
| **Record Label** | The label that released it |
| **Composer** | Who wrote the music and melody |
| **Lyricist** | Who wrote the lyrics |
| **Producer** | Who oversaw the recording |
| **Related Songs** | Other songs linked by shared credits or context |

## Actions on a song page

Signed-in members can take these actions on published song pages:

- **Suggest an edit**: correct a section, such as title, credits, release info, genres, labels, artwork, lyrics, notes, or links.
- **Claim credit**: if your account links to an artist profile, select the roles you did.
- **Share**: copy the song URL or use the share control.

Edits are credited to your account and shown in the song's activity. To add a new song, use [Add a song](/contribute/adding-songs). New songs stay under review before they go public.

### A Note on Non-Latin Titles

Some titles use Arabic script, Jawi, or other non-Latin scripts. Diskograf stores these titles as written. URLs use plain ASCII slugs, so every song has a stable link.

## Credits Explained

Each person is listed under the role they did. One person can have several roles. For example, a singer-songwriter can be **Lead Performer**, **Composer**, and **Lyricist**.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Lead performer">
    The **Lead Performer** is the main recording artist of the song.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Writers">
    - **Composer**: writes the music, including the melody, chords, and musical structure.
    - **Lyricist**: writes the song's words.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Production">
    - **Producer**: leads the song or album project and oversees recording, personnel, finances, and delivery.
    - **Arranger**: shapes the musical parts and may also handle programming.
    - **Mixing Engineer**: blends the recorded elements into the final mix.
    - **Mastering Engineer**: finalizes the track for release.
    - **Recording**: captures the source performance at the best possible quality.
    - **Strings Arranger**: writes or shapes the ensemble string parts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Musicians">
    - **Backing Vocals**: provides supporting vocals and choruses.
    - **Guitars**: plays guitar and other picked string instruments, such as ukulele, banjo, gambus, oud, bouzouki, or sitar.
    - **Bass**: plays bass, including fingered, slapped, picked, or fretless parts.
    - **Keys/Synths**: plays keyboard, piano, and keyboard-style instruments such as keytar, melodian, accordion, or harmonium.
    - **Drums**: plays the drum kit.
    - **Strings**: plays bowed string instruments such as violin, viola, cello, double bass, or erhu.
    - **Woodwinds**: plays instruments such as flute, piccolo, clarinet, oboe, bassoon, pan flute, ocarina, or recorder.
    - **Brass/Horns**: plays or contributes to the brass and horn section, including trumpet, saxophone, trombone, French or English horn, and tuba.
    - **Percussions**: plays percussion instruments such as bongos, congas, cajón, or other percussion parts.
    - **Demo Singer**: records the preliminary vocal before the final vocal.
    - **Other Sessionist**: plays session instruments that do not fit another musician role.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Visual">
    - **Music Video**: directs, edits, or works on the music video crew.
    - **Artwork**: designs the cover artwork for a single or album.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
