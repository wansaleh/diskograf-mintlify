Every song on Diskograf is more than just a title and a name — it's a complete record of who made it. From the composer who wrote the melody to the producer who shaped the final mix, Diskograf surfaces the full story behind Malaysian music, one song at a time.

## The Song Catalogue

The complete song listing lives at [diskograf.com/songs](https://diskograf.com/songs). From there you can:

- **Browse recent additions** — the listing surfaces newly added and updated songs, so frequent visitors can keep up with what the community has been contributing.
- **Use the search bar** — type a title or phrase to search across the entire catalogue. The search works from any page on the site, not just the song listing.
- **Explore curated lists** — editors and contributors organise songs into themed collections, which appear throughout the site as a way to discover music around a specific era, genre, or theme.

## Song Pages

Each song has its own dedicated page at `diskograf.com/song/{id}-{slug}`. For example: [diskograf.com/song/20926-tersakiti](https://diskograf.com/song/20926-tersakiti).

A song page brings together everything catalogued about that release:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The song's canonical title, including non-Latin scripts where applicable |
| **Release Year** | The year the song was released |
| **Performing Artist(s)** | The artist or artists who recorded the song |
| **Record Label** | The label that released it |
| **Composer** | Who wrote the music and melody |
| **Lyricist** | Who wrote the lyrics |
| **Producer** | Who oversaw the recording |
| **Related Songs** | Other songs linked by shared credits or context |

### A Note on Non-Latin Titles

Some Malaysian songs have titles in Arabic script, Jawi, or other non-Latin writing systems. Diskograf stores these titles faithfully while using stable, ASCII-safe slugs in the URL so that every song has a reliable, shareable address regardless of the script used in the title.

## Credits Explained

Song credits are at the heart of what Diskograf does. Here's what each credit role means:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Composer">
    The **composer** wrote the music — the melody, chords, and musical structure of the song. A song can have more than one composer if the music was co-written.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Lyricist">
    The **lyricist** wrote the words. In Malaysian music, the composer and lyricist are often different people, and Diskograf tracks them separately. Some songs list the same person in both roles.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Producer">
    The **producer** oversaw the recording and production process — shaping the arrangement, directing the session, and delivering the final recording. Production credits help you trace the work of influential behind-the-scenes figures across different artists and eras.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Performer">
    The **performer** is the recording artist or artists who sang or played on the track. A song can credit multiple performers, for example in a duet or ensemble recording.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Curated Lists

Beyond individual song pages, Diskograf supports curated lists — themed collections assembled by editors and contributors. Lists can be built around anything: a particular decade, a musical genre, a record label's golden era, or a personal selection.

Song artwork can be used as the cover image for a list, giving each collection a visual identity drawn directly from the music it features.

Look out for featured lists on the homepage and throughout the catalogue — they're one of the best ways to discover songs you haven't heard before.

<Note>
  Every song page shows a selection of related songs at the bottom of the page. This is a quick way to keep exploring — related songs share credits, context, or style with the one you're looking at, and they often lead somewhere unexpected.
</Note>

If a song you're looking for isn't in the catalogue yet, you can help build the database. See [Adding Songs](/contribute/adding-songs) to learn how to contribute.
