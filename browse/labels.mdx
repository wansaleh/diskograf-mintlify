Behind every Malaysian song is a label that believed in it enough to release it. Diskograf catalogues the full landscape of Malaysian record labels — from major imprints with hundreds of releases to independent operations that defined a generation — so you can explore the music industry from the business end as well as the artistic one.

## Label Pages

Each record label in the Diskograf catalogue has its own profile page at `diskograf.com/label/{id}-{slug}`. For example, Rocketfuel Entertainment's page lives at [diskograf.com/label/153-rocketfuel-entertainment](https://diskograf.com/label/153-rocketfuel-entertainment).

## Searching by Label

You can filter your song search by label directly from the search bar using the `label:` filter (shorthand: `l:`). This scopes your results to songs released under that label.

```text
label:sony
```

```text
l:rocketfuel
```

You can also combine the label filter with other search terms. For example, to find ballads released on Suria Records:

```text
ballad label:suria
```

Or to find all releases from a label in a specific decade:

```text
label:arteffects decade:1990
```

See the [Search reference](/browse/search) for the full list of available filters and how to combine them.

<Tip>
  Label names in the search filter support partial matches — you don't need to type the full company name. Searching `l:rocket` will surface results from Rocketfuel Entertainment without needing to type the complete name.
</Tip>
