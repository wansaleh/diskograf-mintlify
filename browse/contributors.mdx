Every song, credit, and artist profile exists because someone added it. The Contributors page shows the community behind that work.

## The contributor overview

The top of the Contributors page includes:

- **Archive Pulse** — public member and recent-contribution totals
- **Active People** — members with recent activity
- **All-time leaderboard** — members with the highest contribution totals

The contributor directory follows these sections.

## The contributor directory

Search the directory by display name or username. You can filter the results by:

- **Everyone**
- **Fans**
- **Artists**
- **Contributors**

Sort by recent activity, newest members, contribution count, or name. The directory is paginated.

Each entry links to a public profile. A profile shows the display name, username, image, activity, Loved Songs, and any linked artist identity.

## Public activity and contribution history

Public profiles show an **Activity** view. The profile owner can open **Manage contributions** to search and edit their songs. Pending songs stay private to the owner during review.

The [Public API](/developers/public-api) also provides a paginated contribution-history endpoint for public profiles.

## Becoming a contributor

Any registered account can contribute. Sign in with Google and finish onboarding. Then use **Add a song**, **Suggest an edit**, or **Claim credit**.

New songs are reviewed before they go public. Published edits are credited to your account and shown in activity.

Start with [How to Contribute](/contribute/overview) if you're new.
