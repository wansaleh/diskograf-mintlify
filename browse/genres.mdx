Genres are one way to explore the catalogue. Open a genre from the Genres index, or click a genre tag on a song page.

## Finding genres

Open the Genres index from the main menu. Each genre links to its own page.

Song and artist pages also show genre tags. Click a tag to open its page.

## What a genre page shows

A genre page includes:

- The genre name and song count
- A grid of songs tagged with that genre
- Pagination for browsing more songs

Genre pages have no artist directory, label roster, or role filter. Use the [Songs](/browse/songs) page for more filters.

## Combine a genre with other filters

On the Songs page, select one or more genres in the filter bar. Then search with tokens such as `artist:`, `label:`, `year:`, and `decade:`.

For example, select **Pop** in the genre filter and search:

```text
artist:sheila decade:2000
```

This shows Pop songs from the 2000s by an artist matching "sheila".

## Missing or incorrect genres

If a genre is wrong or missing, open the song and choose **Suggest an edit**. See [Credits](/contribute/credits) for the standards.
