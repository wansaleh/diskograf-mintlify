Diskograf brings together the full creative history of Malaysian music under one roof. Whether you're tracing the decades-long career of a legend or discovering an emerging act, every artist in the catalogue has a dedicated profile page where you can explore their releases, roles, and collaborators.

## Finding an Artist

There are three ways to get to an artist on Diskograf.

- **Browse the artist listing** — Head to [diskograf.com/artists](https://diskograf.com/artists) for a full, scrollable directory of every artist in the catalogue.
- **Search from the nav bar** — Type an artist's name into the search bar from any page. Use the `artist:` filter to scope your results directly to artists — for example, `artist:yuna` — and the matching profile will appear at the top of your results.
- **Navigate directly by URL** — If you know the artist's ID or slug, go straight to their page using the pattern `diskograf.com/artist/{id}-{slug}`. For example, Siti Nurhaliza's profile lives at [diskograf.com/artist/2-siti-nurhaliza](https://diskograf.com/artist/2-siti-nurhaliza).

### Navigating the Discography

When an artist's discography spans multiple pages, you can move through them quickly using your keyboard:

- **Left / Right arrow keys** — jump one page at a time
- **Shift + Left / Right arrow keys** — jump ten pages at a time

This makes it fast to scan through deep catalogues without reaching for the mouse.

## Genre Filtering

Every artist page supports genre filtering, letting you narrow the discography down to the style you're interested in. Select a genre from the tab bar on the artist's page, or append the `?genre=` parameter directly to the URL.

```text
diskograf.com/artist/{id}-{slug}?genre=ballad
```

For example, to view only ballad releases on Siti Nurhaliza's page:

```text
diskograf.com/artist/2-siti-nurhaliza?genre=ballad
```

Genre tags are assigned at the song level, so the filter reflects the full range of styles an artist has worked across throughout their career.

## Artist Aliases

Many Malaysian artists are known by more than one name — a full name, a shortened stage name, a romanised spelling, or an older name used earlier in their career. Diskograf cross-references these aliases so that a search for any known variant surfaces the correct profile.

You don't need to know the exact canonical name to find an artist. If an alias exists in the system, it will resolve to the right page.

<Tip>
  When searching for an artist with a multi-word name, wrap the name in quotes to make sure Diskograf treats it as a single term: `artist:"siti nurhaliza"`. Without quotes, each word is treated as a separate search token and your results may be broader than expected.
</Tip>

For the full search syntax — including how to combine artist filters with year, decade, and label filters — see the [Search reference](/browse/search).
