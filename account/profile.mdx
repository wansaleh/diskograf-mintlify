Your Diskograf profile is your home in Malaysia's music credits community. It shows who you are, what you've been up to, and — if you're an artist — connects to your artist presence in the database. Keeping your profile up to date helps other users know who they're engaging with.

## Your Public Profile

Every Diskograf account comes with a public profile at `diskograf.com/{username}`. Anyone visiting that URL can see your display name, profile photo, recent activity, and any artist profile you have claimed. This public presence is what makes Diskograf a community rather than just a database — your contributions and taste are part of the record.

## Updating Your Profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to your account">
    Go to [diskograf.com](https://diskograf.com) and sign in using your email address or Google account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your profile settings">
    Navigate to your account settings or visit your profile page directly. Look for the edit or settings option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit your details">
    Update your display name, username, or profile photo as needed. Changes are previewed before you save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Confirm and save. Your updated profile will be visible to other users immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Profile Photo

During onboarding, Diskograf asks you to upload a profile photo. This helps other community members recognise you and adds a personal touch to your contributions and activity. If you skipped that step or want to refresh your photo later, you can upload or change it at any time from your profile settings.

## Username

Your username is more than a login detail — it becomes part of your permanent public URL at `diskograf.com/{username}`. Choose something clear and memorable, ideally something you'll be happy to share publicly.

Keep in mind: **changing your username will change your public URL**. Any links others have saved to your profile under the old username will no longer work, so it's worth choosing carefully from the start.

<Note>
  If you are an artist, claiming and verifying your artist profile is a separate process from managing your user account profile. Your artist profile represents your presence in the music credits database, while your account profile represents you as a member of the Diskograf community. See the [Artist Identity — Claiming a Profile](/identity/claiming-profile) guide to get started.
</Note>
