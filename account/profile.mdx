Your profile is your public identity on Diskograf. It shows who you are and what you contributed. For artists, it also links to your artist identity.

## Your Public Profile

Every account has a public profile at `diskograf.com/{username}`. Anyone can see your display name, image, activity, and Loved Songs. Artist profiles also show the artist's works and credits. Your **Under Review** view is private.

## Updating Your Profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to your account">
    Go to [diskograf.com](https://diskograf.com) and select **Sign in with Google**. This also creates an account for new members.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your profile settings">
    Open your profile and select **Edit profile**. Or open `/profile` directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit your details">
    Update your username, display name, real name, image, artist link, aliases, biography, location, birthday, and social links. Artist profiles can also list collaborators and music-service links.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Save. Other users see the changes right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Profile Photo

Onboarding requires a profile image. You can replace or remove it later in profile settings.

## Username

Your username is part of your public URL: `diskograf.com/{username}`. Pick something clear and easy to share.

**Changing your username changes your URL.** Old links to your profile will stop working. Choose carefully.

<Note>
  Artists set up their public identity by linking an artist profile. Search for your artist or create one in profile settings. See [Claiming a Profile](/identity/claiming-profile) for the rules.
</Note>
