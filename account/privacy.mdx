This page is a plain summary of the data Diskograf collects and why. It also shows how to contact us about your rights.

## What Diskograf Collects

Diskograf collects a small amount of information to operate the service:

- **Account data**: your email, first name, and last name from sign-up. Your profile photo if you upload one.
- **Usage data**: pages you visit, time on site, browser type and version, IP address, and device identifiers. This is collected automatically.
- **Google sign-in data**: if you sign in with Google, Diskograf receives your name, email, and related Google account activity for sign-in.

## How Your Data Is Used

Diskograf uses the information it collects to:

- Provide and maintain the Diskograf service
- Manage your account and keep it secure
- Contact you about important updates, service changes, or support matters
- Maintain artist identity links and investigate suspected impersonation
- Improve the service through analytics and usage insights

We do not sell your personal data.

## Cookies

Diskograf uses cookies and web beacons to run the service and remember your preferences:

- **Session cookies** (necessary): required for the service to work. They expire when you close your browser.
- **Cookie preference cookies** (persistent): remember if you accepted the cookie policy, so you are not asked again.
- **Functionality cookies** (persistent): remember preferences such as display or language settings.

You can control cookies in your browser. Blocking necessary cookies may break parts of the service.

## Your Rights

You can ask what personal data Diskograf holds, ask for corrections, or ask for deletion. Contact us:

**Email**: [diskografcom@gmail.com](mailto:diskografcom@gmail.com)

We will reply as soon as we can.
