Loved songs is your personal collection of Malaysian music. Use it to mark the tracks that matter to you.

## Saving a Song

On any song page, click the **heart icon**. The icon fills to confirm. You can love any number of songs.

## Managing Your Loved Songs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your loved songs page">
    Open your loved songs page from your profile. It lists every saved song in your order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove a song">
    Click the **remove option** next to a song. It leaves the list at once. You can re-add it from the song page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reorder your songs">
    **Drag and drop** songs to reorder them. The new order saves automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your Loved Songs Page

Your loved songs are part of your public profile. Visitors to `diskograf.com/{username}` can see the songs and their order.

This list is always public. There is no option to make it private.

<Tip>
  Use loved songs as a to-do list. Love a song with missing credits to bookmark it. Come back later to research and add the credits.
</Tip>
