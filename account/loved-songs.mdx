Your loved songs list is your personal collection within the Diskograf archive — a curated shelf of Malaysian music that means something to you. Whether you're a lifelong fan of a certain era or discovering new artists through the database, loved songs lets you mark the tracks that matter and keep them close.

## Saving a Song

From any song page on Diskograf, click the **heart icon** to add that song to your loved songs list. The icon will fill to confirm the song has been saved. There's no limit to how many songs you can love.

## Managing Your Loved Songs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your loved songs page">
    Access your loved songs page through your profile. You'll see a full list of every song you've saved, in the order you've arranged them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove a song">
    To remove a song from your list, click the **remove option** next to that song. It will be taken off your list immediately. You can always re-add it from the song's page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reorder your songs">
    To rearrange your loved songs, **drag and drop** them into the order you prefer. Your new arrangement is saved automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your Loved Songs Page

Your loved songs page is part of your public Diskograf profile. Other users visiting `diskograf.com/{username}` can see the songs you've saved and how you've arranged them. It's a window into your musical taste and a way to connect with others who love the same music.

If you'd rather keep certain listening habits to yourself, be mindful that this list is publicly visible — there is currently no option to make it private.

<Tip>
  Your loved songs list doubles as a personal work queue. If you spot a song with missing or incomplete credits, love it to bookmark it for later — then come back when you're ready to research and contribute the credits. It's a handy way to keep track of everything you want to work on without losing your place in the archive.
</Tip>
