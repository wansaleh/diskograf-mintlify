Diskograf is an index of Malaysian music credits. The community builds it, and it grows every day.

Use it to find who wrote, sang, or produced a song. You can trace a P. Ramlee classic, check a 2000s pop hit, or list your own releases. Every credit lives in one searchable record.

## Trusted by the Malaysian Music Industry

Publishers, labels, and rights organisations use Diskograf for accurate Malaysian credits. Partners include **Universal Music Publishing**, **Sony Music Publishing**, **KRU Music**, and **Rocketfuel Entertainment**. Many independent publishers and labels use it too.
