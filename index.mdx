Diskograf is the definitive index of Malaysian music credits — a living, community-powered database that maps the full story behind every song. Whether you're tracing the composer of a classic P. Ramlee number, verifying the producer of a 2000s pop hit, or cataloguing your own releases as an independent artist, Diskograf brings every credit into one searchable record. From the earliest recordings to today's chart toppers, the archive grows every day through contributions from fans, artists, and music industry professionals across Malaysia.

## Trusted by the Malaysian Music Industry

Diskograf is relied upon by publishers, labels, and rights organisations who need accurate, up-to-date credits for the Malaysian repertoire. Industry partners who use and contribute to the database include **Universal Music Publishing**, **Sony Music Publishing**, **KRU Music**, **Rocketfuel Entertainment**, and many independent publishers and labels across the country. Their trust reflects the accuracy and depth that the Diskograf community works hard to maintain.
