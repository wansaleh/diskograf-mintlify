---
title: "Get Started with Diskograf: Explore Malaysian Music"
sidebarTitle: "Quickstart"
description: "Learn how to explore the Malaysian music catalogue, search for songs and artists, and make your first contribution to Diskograf today."
---

Diskograf is designed to be immediately useful. You can start exploring the Malaysian music catalogue right now without an account, and when you're ready to give back to the archive, signing up takes less than a minute. This guide walks you through the essentials — from your first search to your first contribution.

<Tip>
  You can browse the full catalogue without an account — signing up is only needed to contribute. Dive in and get a feel for the database before you register.
</Tip>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Explore the Catalogue">
    Head to [diskograf.com](https://diskograf.com) to start browsing. The homepage surfaces recently added songs and active contributors so you can see what the community is working on. From there, navigate directly to an **artist page** to see their full discography and genre breakdown, or visit a **label page** to browse every release associated with a record company. Each song page lists the full credit roll — composer, lyricist, producer, performer, and more — so you always know the complete story behind a track.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for a Song or Artist">
    Use the search bar at the top of any page to find exactly what you're looking for. A plain text query searches song titles, but Diskograf's search syntax gives you much more precision. For example, to find songs with "cinta" in the title by Siti Nurhaliza released in the 2000s, type:

    ```
    cinta artist:siti decade:2000
    ```

    You can combine as many filters as you need. Use `label:name` to search by record label, `year:2020` for an exact release year, or `decade:1990` for any year within a decade. See the [full search syntax reference](/browse/search) for every available filter and shorthand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an Account">
    When you're ready to contribute, sign up for a free Diskograf account. Go to the sign-up page and choose either **email and password** or **Continue with Google** — both options take under a minute. During onboarding, you'll be prompted to set a display name, a username (which becomes your public profile URL at `diskograf.com/yourusername`), and upload a profile photo. You can update all of these later from your account settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make Your First Contribution">
    Once you're logged in, find a song that has a missing or incorrect credit and make your first edit. Open the song page and select **Edit Credits**. You can add a new credit line — for example, attaching a lyricist who wasn't listed — or correct an existing entry that has a misspelling or wrong attribution. Submit your change with a short note explaining your source, and it will enter the review queue. When it's approved, your contribution becomes a permanent part of the archive and is credited to your profile.
  </Step>
</Steps>
