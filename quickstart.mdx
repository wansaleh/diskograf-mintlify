You can explore Diskograf right away, without an account. Signing up takes less than a minute when you want to contribute. This guide covers your first search and your first contribution.

<Tip>
  You only need an account to contribute. Browse first to get a feel for the database.
</Tip>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Explore the Catalogue">
    Go to [diskograf.com](https://diskograf.com). The homepage shows recently added songs and active contributors. Open an **artist page** to see their discography and genres. Open a **label page** to see every release from that company. Each song page lists all credits, such as composer, lyricist, producer, and performer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for a Song or Artist">
    Use the search bar at the top of any page. Plain text searches song titles. Filters make your search more precise. For example, this finds 2000s songs by Siti Nurhaliza with "cinta" in the title:

    ```
    cinta artist:siti decade:2000
    ```

    You can combine any number of filters. Use `label:name` for a record label, `year:2020` for an exact year, or `decade:1990` for a decade. See the [full search syntax reference](/browse/search) for every filter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an Account">
    Open the sign-in page and choose **Sign in with Google**. This also creates your account if you are new. During onboarding, you:

    - Upload a profile image.
    - Choose a display name and username.
    - Select **Fan** or **Artist**. Artists link an existing artist profile, or create one.

    Your username becomes your profile URL: `diskograf.com/yourusername`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Contribution">
    After you sign in, open a song page and pick an action:

    - **Claim credit** adds your artist identity and roles to an existing song.
    - **Suggest an edit** corrects a published song.
    - **Add a song** adds a missing song. Check for duplicates first, then fill in the form.

    New songs are reviewed before they go public. Track them in the **Under Review** section of your profile. See [Adding Songs](/contribute/adding-songs) and [Credits](/contribute/credits) for details.
  </Step>
</Steps>
