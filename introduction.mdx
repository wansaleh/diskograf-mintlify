Diskograf is a community-powered music credits database dedicated entirely to Malaysian music. It exists to answer a deceptively simple question: who made this song? Behind every track is a web of contributors — the songwriter who crafted the melody, the lyricist who chose every word, the producer who shaped the sound, and the performer who brought it to life. Diskograf captures all of them in a single, searchable, ever-growing archive. Spanning every genre and every generation of the Malaysian music industry, it is built by the people who love and work in Malaysian music, and it belongs to them.

## Who Uses Diskograf

Diskograf serves a wide range of people, all united by an interest in Malaysian music:

- **Fans and listeners** who want to know the story behind a song they love — who wrote it, who produced it, and what else those people have made.
- **Working musicians** who need to verify credits, research collaborators, or ensure their own contributions are accurately documented.
- **Music industry professionals** — A&R managers, sync licensing teams, and rights administrators — who rely on Diskograf for accurate repertoire data.
- **Publishers and labels** such as Universal Music Publishing, Sony Music Publishing, KRU Music, and Rocketfuel Entertainment, who use the database to cross-reference and maintain their catalogues.
- **Collectors and archivists** dedicated to preserving the history of Malaysian recorded music across formats and eras.

## A Living Archive

Diskograf is not a finished product — it is a living archive that grows more complete with every contribution. When you add a missing lyricist to a 1980s ballad, correct a misspelled producer credit, or submit an entirely new entry for an obscure regional release, you are adding a permanent record to the collective memory of Malaysian music. The community checks and improves each other's contributions over time, so the archive becomes more accurate the more people use it. No single institution could build this alone; Diskograf is built by everyone.

<Note>
  Diskograf focuses exclusively on Malaysian music across all genres and generations — from traditional folk and film soundtrack recordings to pop, rock, hip-hop, and contemporary indie releases. If it's part of the Malaysian music story, it belongs here.
</Note>
