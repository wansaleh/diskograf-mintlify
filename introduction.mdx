Diskograf is a community music credits database for Malaysian music. It answers one question: who made this song?

Many people make a track. A songwriter writes the melody, and a lyricist writes the words. A producer shapes the sound, and a performer sings it. Diskograf records all of them in one searchable archive. It covers every genre and generation, and the people who love Malaysian music build it.

## Who Uses Diskograf

Diskograf serves anyone interested in Malaysian music:

- **Fans and listeners** who want to know who wrote or produced a song, and what else they made.
- **Working musicians** who check credits, research collaborators, or document their own work.
- **Industry professionals** such as A&R managers, sync licensing teams, and rights administrators who need accurate repertoire data.
- **Publishers and labels** such as Universal Music Publishing, Sony Music Publishing, KRU Music, and Rocketfuel Entertainment, who cross-check their catalogues.
- **Collectors and archivists** who preserve Malaysian recorded music across formats and eras.

## A Living Archive

Diskograf is never finished. Each contribution makes it more complete.

You might add a missing lyricist, fix a misspelled producer, or add a rare regional release. Each one becomes a permanent record. The community checks and improves each other's work, so accuracy grows with use. No single institution could build this alone.

<Note>
  Diskograf covers only Malaysian music, in all genres and eras. That includes folk, film soundtracks, pop, rock, hip-hop, and indie. If it is part of Malaysian music history, it belongs here.
</Note>
