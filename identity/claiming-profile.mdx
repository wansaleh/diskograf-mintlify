Artists can link their account to the artist identity that represents them. The link connects your account to your works. It also lets you claim your credits on existing songs.

## Who can link an artist profile

Link only a profile that represents you. Do not claim another artist or a namesake. Do not claim an artist you manage without their own authorization. If another account already holds the link, do not try to get around it.

## Link your profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in and open your profile settings">
    Sign in with Google, then open **Edit profile** from your public profile. New accounts go through onboarding first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Artist">
    In the artist section, choose **Artist**. You need an artist identity to claim song credits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for your artist profile">
    Search by your stage name or artist name. Select the profile that is you. Check the name, aliases, group or solo type, and discography.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the identity acknowledgement">
    Confirm the profile is yours, then save. Diskograf links the profile to your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Not listed? Create a new artist identity. Search first to avoid duplicates.
</Note>

## After you link

Your public profile can show your works, credits, genres, aliases, biography, collaborators, and social links. Update them in [profile settings](/account/profile).

To add yourself to an existing song, open it and choose **Claim credit**. Select your roles and confirm.

Linking is not staff verification. Diskograf has no verification badge and no claim review queue.

## If you selected the wrong artist

If no result clearly matches you, stop before saving. If you linked the wrong profile, fix the link in profile settings before you claim credits.

To report impersonation, or a profile linked to someone else, use [Contact](/support/contact). Include the profile URL and your reason.
