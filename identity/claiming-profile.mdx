Diskograf lets real Malaysian artists take ownership of their catalogue entry — turning a community-created record into your official presence on the platform. Claiming your profile means your account is directly linked to your artist page, giving you the ability to manage your information and be recognised as the verified artist behind the work.

## Who Can Claim a Profile

Only the actual artist whose name appears on the profile may submit a claim. This is strictly for genuine identity claims — not for fan accounts, managers, or any third party acting without explicit authorisation from the artist. If you are not the artist named on the profile, you may not claim it on their behalf.

## How to Claim Your Profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create or sign in to your account">
    You need a Diskograf account to claim a profile. If you are signing up for the first time, indicate that you are an artist during the onboarding process — you will also be asked to upload a profile photo at this stage. If you already have an account, sign in and ensure your account type is set to artist.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your catalogue entry">
    Search for your artist name on Diskograf. Your profile may already exist in the catalogue if you have been credited on songs previously documented by the community. Browse the search results to locate the correct entry that corresponds to your identity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit an identity claim">
    From your artist profile page, use the claim option to link your account to the catalogue entry. This signals to Diskograf that you are asserting ownership of that profile as the genuine artist.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Await verification">
    Diskograf reviews every identity claim to protect the integrity of the archive. You may be contacted at the email address on your account for additional confirmation. The review timeline varies, so keep an eye on your inbox and notifications.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage your profile">
    Once your claim is approved, your account is linked to the artist profile and you can manage it directly. Update your bio, profile photo, and other details to reflect your authentic presence on the platform.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If your artist profile does not yet exist in the catalogue, you can create a new one during the claim process. You are not limited to claiming pre-existing entries — Diskograf supports both claiming existing profiles and establishing new ones.
</Note>

## What Happens After You Claim

Once your claim is approved, the profile belongs to you. You can update your profile photo, bio, and other details at any time to keep your page accurate and current. Your profile is marked as claimed by the verified artist, giving fans and fellow music lovers confidence that they are seeing information you have endorsed directly.

Owning your profile also means you are the authoritative voice on your own catalogue entry — a meaningful distinction in a community-powered archive built on the contributions of Malaysian music fans and creators alike.
