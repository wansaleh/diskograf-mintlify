Diskograf takes artist identity seriously. Every claim submitted through the platform goes through a verification process designed to protect both artists and the integrity of the catalogue. This ensures that the archive remains a trustworthy record of Malaysian music — one where the identities behind the profiles are genuine.

## Why Verification Matters

The archive is only as trustworthy as the identities behind it. When fans, researchers, and industry professionals look up an artist on Diskograf, they deserve confidence that the profile they are reading is authentic. Verification protects artists from impersonation and ensures that the information on each page carries the endorsement of the real person it represents. Without this process, the credibility of the entire catalogue is at risk.

## The Verification Process

After you submit a claim, here is what happens:

- **Diskograf reviews your claim** against available information in the catalogue and your account details. The team assesses whether the claim is consistent with the identity on record.
- **You may be contacted** at the email address associated with your Diskograf account for additional confirmation. This could include questions about your catalogue, your background, or other details that help establish your identity.
- **The review timeline varies** depending on the volume of claims and the complexity of your case. There is no fixed turnaround, so be patient and watch your inbox.
- **You will be notified of the outcome** once a decision has been made. Approved claims link your Diskograf account directly to the artist profile.

<Tip>
  Make sure your account email is one that Diskograf can use to reach you during the verification process. Using an unmonitored or temporary email address may delay or prevent successful verification.
</Tip>

## Claim Outcomes

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Approved">
    Your account is linked to the artist profile. You can now manage the profile directly — update your bio, profile photo, and other details — and you are recognised on Diskograf as the verified artist behind that catalogue entry. Your profile will reflect your authenticated ownership, giving fans and the wider community confidence in the information displayed.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Rejected or Under Review">
    Your claim may be rejected if your identity cannot be confirmed from the information provided. This does not necessarily mean the case is closed. You can re-submit your claim with additional supporting information by contacting Diskograf at **diskografcom@gmail.com**. Explain your situation and include any relevant details that help establish your identity as the artist in question.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Keeping Your Identity Secure

Once your profile is verified, it is your responsibility to keep your account secure. Do not share your Diskograf login credentials with anyone else — not managers, collaborators, or fans. Your account is the key to your verified presence on the platform, and sharing access puts that verification at risk.

If you notice any suspicious activity on your account or believe someone may have gained unauthorised access, report it to Diskograf immediately at **diskografcom@gmail.com** so the team can investigate and help you regain control.
