Linking connects a member account to a public artist identity. It is an account link, not a verification program.

## What linking means

When you confirm an artist identity in onboarding or profile settings:

- Your account is linked to that artist profile.
- Your public profile can show the artist's works and credits.
- You can use **Claim credit** to add your artist identity and roles to existing songs.
- You can update the profile details that belong to your linked artist identity.

The link does not mean Diskograf verified your identity. There is no verification badge, claim review queue, claim status page, or approval notice.

## Use the identity acknowledgement carefully

Confirm only an artist identity that is you. Before saving, check the name, aliases, group or solo type, and discography. If results are unclear, stop and check first.

If the artist does not exist, search again with other spellings before you create one. Duplicate profiles make credits confusing.

## Keep your profile accurate

After linking, update your artist details in [profile settings](/account/profile). These may include:

- Stage name and aliases
- Group or solo type
- Biography and primary job
- Location, birthplace, and birth date
- Collaborators
- Profile image and social links

To fix a published song, use **Suggest an edit** on the song page. To add your role to an existing song, use **Claim credit**.

## Report an identity problem

Never claim another person's artist profile. If an account impersonates an artist, or the wrong account is linked, report it through [Contact](/support/contact).

See [Claiming a Profile](/identity/claiming-profile) for linking steps. See [Reporting Artist Impersonation](/identity/impersonation) for reporting.
