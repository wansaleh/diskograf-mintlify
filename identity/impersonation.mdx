Impersonation means claiming to be an artist you are not. It is strictly prohibited. Each artist profile must link to the real person. False claims harm the real artists and the catalogue.

## What Counts as Impersonation

Impersonation is claiming or creating an artist profile as someone other than yourself. Famous and lesser-known artists are protected equally. Impersonation includes:

- **Claiming another real artist's profile** when you are not that artist
- **Creating a fake profile** with another artist's name or likeness
- **Acting for an artist without explicit permission** from that artist

## Consequences of Impersonation

If Diskograf finds a false claim, it may:

- **Investigate** the account, the claim, and related activity
- **Reject or reverse the claim** and restore the profile
- **Suspend or permanently ban** the account
- **Refer the matter under the law**: in Malaysia, deliberate impersonation can be identity theft and may be a crime

<Warning>
  Deliberate false claims may break Malaysian law. Diskograf acts on all credible reports and may escalate them.
</Warning>

## How to Report Impersonation

If someone is impersonating you or another artist, report it quickly:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Contact Diskograf by email">
    Email **diskografcom@gmail.com**. The Diskograf team monitors this address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Include the affected profile URL">
    Give the full URL of the affected artist profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe why you believe the claim is fraudulent">
    Explain why you think the claim is false. Add context, such as your link to the artist, proof of the real artist's identity, or details about the impersonator.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Await Diskograf's response">
    Diskograf investigates and acts on its findings. It contacts you if it needs more information.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Honest mistakes are treated differently from deliberate impersonation. If you made a claim in error, email **diskografcom@gmail.com** to explain. Mistakes can often be fixed without penalty.
</Note>
