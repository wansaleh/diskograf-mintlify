A Diskograf account is your identity in the community. It lets you contribute credits, correct errors, save songs you love, and build curated lists — and if you're a recording artist, it gives you the tools to claim and verify your own catalogue profile. Accounts are free and take less than a minute to create.

<Note>
  You must be at least 18 years old to create a Diskograf account, in accordance with Diskograf's Terms of Service.
</Note>

## Sign Up

To create your account, go to the sign-up page and choose one of two methods:

- **Email and password** — enter your email address, choose a password, and verify your email address via the confirmation link sent to your inbox.
- **Google sign-in** — select **Continue with Google** to authenticate using your existing Google account. No separate password is needed.

During onboarding, you'll be asked to upload a **profile photo** as part of setting up your public identity on Diskograf. You can skip this step and add a photo later from your account settings, but adding one during onboarding helps your profile feel complete to other contributors from the start.

## Account Types

Diskograf has two account types. You choose your path during sign-up, and you can upgrade from a fan account to an artist account at any time.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Fan Account">
    A fan account is the default for anyone joining Diskograf as a listener, researcher, or enthusiast. With a fan account you can:

    - **Browse** the full catalogue of Malaysian songs, artists, and labels without any restrictions
    - **Contribute credits** — add new songs, attach missing credits, and correct existing information
    - **Save songs** you love to your personal library for quick reference
    - **Build lists** — create and publish curated playlists and themed collections that other users can follow and discover
    - **Follow** other contributors and artists to stay updated on their activity

    A fan account is the right choice for the vast majority of Diskograf users. You don't need to be an artist to be a valued member of the community.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Artist Account">
    An artist account includes everything a fan account offers, plus the ability to engage with Diskograf as a verified recording artist:

    - **Everything in a fan account** — full browsing, contributing, saving, and list-building
    - **Claim an artist profile** — link your Diskograf account to your artist page so that your profile is marked as the authoritative source for your catalogue information
    - **Verified identity badge** — once your claim is approved, your artist profile displays a verified badge that signals to fans, publishers, and industry professionals that the information is accurate and artist-approved
    - **Manage your catalogue** — review and approve credits attached to your songs, and ensure your discography is complete and correctly attributed

    To set up an artist account and claim your profile, see [Claiming Your Artist Profile](/identity/claiming-profile).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Profile Setup

Your public profile on Diskograf is how the community knows you. Here's what you can configure:

- **Display name** — the name shown next to your contributions and on your profile page. This can be your real name, an alias, or anything you prefer.
- **Profile photo** — uploaded during onboarding or updated at any time in your account settings. A recognisable photo makes it easier for collaborators and fans to find you.
- **Username** — your unique handle on Diskograf. Your public profile URL is `diskograf.com/yourusername`. Choose carefully — while usernames can be changed, your previous URL will no longer redirect once updated.

To update any of these details after sign-up, visit your [profile settings](/account/profile).
