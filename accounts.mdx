A Diskograf account lets you contribute credits, fix errors, save songs, and keep a public profile. Artists can link their account to an artist profile during onboarding. Accounts are free.

<Note>
  You must be at least 18 to create an account, under Diskograf's Terms of Service.
</Note>

## Sign Up

Open the sign-in page and select **Sign in with Google**. This also creates your account if you are new. There is no separate email and password sign-up.

On first sign-in, onboarding asks you to:

- Upload a **profile image**. This is required to finish.
- Choose a username and display name.
- Select **Fan** or **Artist**.

If you choose **Artist**, find your artist profile or create one. Confirm it is yours before you save.

## Account Types

You pick one profile type during onboarding. You can start as a Fan and link an artist identity later in profile settings.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Fan Account">
    A fan account is the default for listeners, researchers, and enthusiasts. You can:

    - **Browse** all songs, artists, and labels
    - **Contribute credits**: add songs, add missing credits, and fix errors
    - **Save songs** to your public Loved Songs page
    - **Keep a public profile** with a name, image, and activity

    Most users choose a fan account. You do not need to be an artist to contribute.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Artist Account">
    An artist account has everything a fan account has, plus a public artist identity:

    - **Link an existing artist profile** or create a new one during onboarding
    - **Keep your artist details up to date** from your profile settings
    - **Claim credit** on songs you worked on, with the roles you did

    To link an artist identity, see [Claiming Your Artist Profile](/identity/claiming-profile).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Profile Setup

You can set these profile details:

- **Display name**: shown on your profile and next to your contributions. Use your real name, an alias, or anything you like.
- **Profile image**: required during onboarding. You can change it in account settings.
- **Username**: your unique handle. Your profile URL is `diskograf.com/yourusername`. You can change it, but the old URL will stop working.

To change these later, go to your [profile settings](/account/profile).
