An artist profile gathers an artist's works and credits. If you are the artist, link it to your account and keep your details up to date.

## What an artist profile can include

A linked artist profile can include:

- **Stage name** and aliases
- **Group or solo type**
- **Profile image**
- **Biography** and primary job
- **Location, birthplace, and birth date**
- **Collaborators** and associated acts
- **Social and music-service links**
- **Works and credit roles** from the catalogue

## Link or create your artist identity

You cannot add any artist from the public directory. To set up your own identity, see [Link Your Artist Profile](/identity/claiming-profile). Search for your artist or create one from onboarding or profile settings.

Search before you create. Check aliases and other spellings to avoid duplicates.

## Update your own profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open profile settings">
    Sign in, open your profile, and choose **Edit profile**. Or open `/profile` directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your artist details">
    Update your stage name, aliases, group type, biography, collaborators, location, and social links.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the changes">
    Save. A stage-name change updates your name in every song credit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Correct song information

Artist details and song data are edited in different places:

- **Suggest an edit** on a published song corrects its title, credits, release info, genres, labels, artwork, lyrics, notes, or links.
- **Claim credit** adds your artist identity and roles to an existing song.
- [Adding Songs](/contribute/adding-songs) covers songs missing from the catalogue.

Regular contributors cannot edit other artists' profiles. Edit only the artist identity linked to your account.
