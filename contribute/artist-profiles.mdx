Every song in Diskograf connects to the people who made it. Artist profiles are the anchor for those connections — they're where an artist's full discography comes together in one place, whether they're a legendary P. Ramlee-era vocalist, a prolific Kuala Lumpur producer, or an independent artist who released one EP and disappeared. Keeping these profiles accurate means every credit on every song points somewhere real and useful.

## What's on an Artist Profile

An artist profile on Diskograf can include:

- **Name** — the primary name the artist is known by
- **Aliases** — other names, stage names, or alternate romanisations the artist has used
- **Genre tags** — the musical genres associated with the artist's work
- **Profile photo** — a representative image for the artist
- **Biography** — background information about the artist's career and history
- **Full discography** — every song in the Diskograf catalogue where the artist appears as a performer
- **Credit roles** — all the songs where the artist appears as a composer, lyricist, or producer, even when they aren't the performing artist

## Creating a New Artist Profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search First">
    Before creating anything, search for the artist to confirm they don't already have a profile. Try the artist's primary name and any known aliases or alternate spellings — Malaysian artists often have both romanised and original-script versions of their names, and a profile may already exist under one of them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the Add Artist Option">
    If you've confirmed the artist isn't in the catalogue, find the **Add artist** option from the navigation or artist directory. This opens the new artist form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the Artist's Name and Aliases">
    Enter the artist's primary name — the one they're most commonly known by. Then add any known aliases in the aliases field. The more aliases you add, the easier it will be for other contributors and fans to find the profile when adding credits or searching the catalogue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Genre Tags">
    Select or add genre tags that describe the artist's music. Tags help surface the artist in genre-filtered searches and give context to visitors exploring the catalogue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a Profile Photo">
    Optionally, upload or link a profile photo. This is not required to create the profile, and you can always add or update the photo later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the Profile">
    Save the new profile. It will be available immediately across Diskograf — other contributors will be able to find and select this artist when adding credits to songs right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Keeping Profiles Accurate

Artist information changes over time — performers adopt new stage names, update their photos, and accumulate new credits. You can edit an existing artist's name, aliases, and profile photo at any time from their artist page.

When you update an artist's name, the change appears immediately on every catalogue card and song page that references them — there's no delay and no need to manually update individual song entries. This means correcting a long-standing name error fixes it everywhere in one step.

## Artist Aliases

Many Malaysian artists perform under multiple names across their careers — a solo name, a group name, an English-language alias, or a shortened version of their name that fans use. Aliases are fully indexed in Diskograf's search, so a fan searching for any version of the name will find the same profile.

When you add aliases to a profile, you're not just tidying up a record — you're making it possible for someone who only knows the artist by one name to discover everything they've ever made under any name. For artists whose names use non-Latin scripts, adding a romanised alias ensures the profile is reachable by contributors who may not be able to type the original script.
