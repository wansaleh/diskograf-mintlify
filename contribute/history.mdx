Diskograf credits each change to the account that made it. Your public profile shows an activity feed. A separate private view lets you manage songs you added or edited.

## Public activity

Open `diskograf.com/{username}/activity` to see a profile's public feed. It can include:

- Adding or removing a song credit
- Changing a song title, release date, artwork, lyrics, notes, genre, label, or streaming link
- Adding or editing a song

The feed shows the song and the type of change. Anyone can view it on a public profile.

## Manage your songs

Sign in and open `diskograf.com/{username}/contributions` to manage your songs. Only you can see this view. It lists songs you added or edited. You can:

- Search by song, artist, label, or year
- Filter by **Pending** or **Published**
- Sort by edited, released, added, or title order
- Open a song and continue editing it

This is a management view, not a public timeline.

## Songs under review

New songs stay private during review. Find them in the **Under Review** view on your profile. You can edit pending songs, but others cannot browse them.

Once public, a song appears in the catalogue and on the credited artists' pages.

## Song activity

Song pages can show the activity for that song. For machine-readable history, use the [Public API](/developers/public-api).

Every edit becomes a public byline once the record is public. Check your source and role before you save.
