title: "View Your Activity and Manage Contributions on Diskograf"
sidebarTitle: "Contribution History"
description: "Understand the difference between your public activity, your private song-management view, and songs that are still under review."
---

Diskograf attributes catalogue changes to the account that made them. Your public profile shows an activity feed, while a separate private view helps you manage songs you added or helped edit.

## Public activity

Open `diskograf.com/{username}/activity` to see the public activity feed for a profile. It can include changes such as:

- Adding or removing a song credit
- Changing a song title, release date, artwork, lyrics, notes, genre, label, or streaming link
- Adding or editing a song

The activity feed identifies the affected song and the type of change. Other visitors can view the activity feed on a public profile.

## Manage your songs

Open `diskograf.com/{username}/contributions` while signed in to manage your own catalogue submissions. This view is private to the profile owner. It includes songs you added or helped edit, with tools to:

- Search by song, artist, label, or year
- Filter by **Pending** or **Published**
- Sort by edited, released, added, or title order
- Open a song and continue editing it

The route is a management view, not a public contribution timeline.

## Songs under review

New songs are private while they are under review. Open the **Under Review** view on your own profile to find pending submissions. Pending songs can be edited, but other visitors cannot use that view to browse them.

When a song becomes public, it appears in the catalogue and in the credited artists' works.

## Song activity

Song pages can show contribution activity for changes made to that record. For a machine-readable version of public contribution history, use the [Public API](/developers/public-api).

Every edit is a public byline once the affected record is public. Check your source and role before you save a change.
