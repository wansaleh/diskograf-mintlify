You need an account to edit a song. Use **Suggest an edit** to correct a published song. Use **Contributions** to manage songs you added or edited.

## Edit a published song

Use **Suggest an edit** when a published song has wrong or missing information.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the song page">
    Search by title or artist, then open the song.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Suggest an edit">
    Select **Suggest an edit**. A section picker opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a section">
    Select one of the available sections:

    - **YouTube Video**
    - **Song Title**
    - **Song Credits**
    - **Streaming Links**
    - **Artwork**
    - **Release Date**
    - **Genres**
    - **Record Label**
    - **Lyrics & Notes**, including song duration
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make and save the correction">
    Update the fields you can verify. Confirm the truthfulness pledge and select **Save**. The change goes live on the song and is credited to you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage your contributions

Use **Contributions** to find songs you added or edited. This private view lets you keep improving a song or check its status.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Contributions">
    Sign in, open your profile, and select **Contributions**. Or choose **Manage contributions** from the account menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the song">
    Search by song, artist, label, or year. Filter by **Pending** or **Published**. Sort by last edited, released, added, or title.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Edit">
    Select **Edit** beside the song. Pick a section and update it. Confirm the truthfulness pledge and save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your songs under review appear in your private **Under Review** view. You can edit them while pending. Others cannot see them until they are published.

## What you can edit

You can correct the title, YouTube video, release info, genres, labels, artwork, streaming links, lyrics, notes, duration, and credits. See [Add and Edit Song Credits](/contribute/credits) for all roles.

Did you do a role missing from a published song? Use [Claim your credit](/identity/claiming-profile). If the song is missing, see [Adding Songs](/contribute/adding-songs).

## Before you save

- Check the song and artist identity before changing anything.
- Use a reliable source: release packaging, official streaming data, or an official artist or label source.
- Change only information you can verify.
- Review the updated fields and credit roles before saving.

Every saved edit is credited to you and shown in the song's activity. See [Contribution History](/contribute/history) for details.
