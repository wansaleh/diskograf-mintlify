---

If a song is missing from Diskograf, you can add it in a few minutes. Whether it's a forgotten B-side from the 1970s or a self-released track from last year, the process is the same — fill in what you know, publish the entry, and the song becomes part of the permanent catalogue.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign In">
    Make sure you're logged into your Diskograf account before you begin. You need an active account to add new entries. If you don't have one yet, [create a free account](/accounts) first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check if the Song Already Exists">
    Before adding anything, search for the song to make sure it isn't already in the catalogue. Use both the song title and the artist name in the search bar — try alternate spellings and romanisations too if the title uses non-Latin script. Duplicate entries create confusion and extra cleanup work, so this step is worth the extra minute.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Add Song Form">
    Once you've confirmed the song is missing, find the **Add a song** option in the navigation menu or from the catalogue page. This opens the song submission form where you'll enter all the details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the Song Details">
    Enter the core details for the song. The more complete you can make this, the more useful the entry will be:

    - **Song title** — enter the title exactly as it appears on the official release
    - **Performing artist(s)** — the artist or artists who recorded the song
    - **Record label** — optional, but strongly recommended if you know it
    - **Release year** — the year the song was officially released or published
    - **Song artwork** — optional; you can upload cover art if you have it

    Fill in as many fields as you can. Fields you leave blank can always be completed later by you or another contributor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Credits">
    Use the credits section to document the full creative team behind the song. You can add:

    - **Composer(s)** — who wrote the music or melody
    - **Lyricist(s)** — who wrote the lyrics
    - **Producer(s)** — who oversaw the recording and production

    As you type a name in the credit search field, already-credited artists appear first in the autocomplete results. This makes it quick to link credits when working on songs from the same artist, producer, or label family. If the person you're looking for isn't in the system yet, you can create a new artist profile from within the form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Once you're satisfied with the details, publish the entry. The song will appear in the catalogue immediately and the performing artist's song count will update right away — no delay, no moderation queue.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After Publishing

Once a song is published, the catalogue card for that song reflects any subsequent edits you make in real time. If you update the song artwork or correct the artist name, those changes appear on the catalogue card straight away — you don't need to wait for a refresh or approval.

You can also return to the song page at any time to add credits you didn't have on hand during the initial publish.

<Note>
  For songs with Arabic script, Jawi, or other non-Latin titles, Diskograf automatically generates a stable ASCII slug for the song's URL. This means the link to the song will always work correctly and can be shared reliably, regardless of how the title is written.
</Note>

<Tip>
  You don't have to have every credit ready before you publish. Add the core details now to get the song into the catalogue, then come back to fill in composer, lyricist, and producer credits once you've tracked them down.
</Tip>

For a detailed guide to adding and editing credits, see [Add and Edit Song Credits](/contribute/credits).
