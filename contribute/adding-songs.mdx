You can submit a song that is missing from Diskograf. New songs are reviewed before they go public. Check for duplicates first, and use the most reliable information you have.

## Choose a song-entry path

After you open **Add a song**, pick a path. Both paths collect the same information and create the same song entry. Both autosave your draft and send it for review.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Classic form">
    The classic form shows the full editor on one page. Use it when you know the details or want to work in any order. Section links let you jump between:

    - YouTube video, title, and credits
    - Artwork and release date
    - Genres and labels
    - Streaming links, lyrics, notes, and duration
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Step-by-step wizard">
    The wizard shows the same editor one step at a time, like a checklist. It shows your progress and checks required sections. A live preview sits beside the form. The last step lets you review the record before you submit.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  You can switch between the two paths at any time. Your draft carries over.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Sign in before you begin. No account? [Create one](/accounts) with **Sign in with Google**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check if the song already exists">
    Search by title and artist. For non-Latin titles, try other spellings and romanisations. If Diskograf shows similar titles while you submit, check them first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Add a song">
    Open **Add a song** from the header or search menu. Choose **Classic form** or **Step-by-step wizard**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the song details">
    Add as much of the following information as you can:

    - **YouTube video**: the video linked to the song
    - **Song title**: the title as it appears on the release
    - **Lead performer**: the primary recording artist
    - **Artwork**: the cover image or other song artwork
    - **Release date**: when the song was released
    - **Genres**: the genres that describe the song
    - **Record label**: the label associated with the release
    - **Streaming links**: Spotify or Apple Music links, when available
    - **Lyrics, notes, and duration**: supporting information for the song

    The editor saves your draft as you work. You can fill optional fields later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add credits">
    Use the credits section to list the creative team. Select the role, then search for the artist and add them. See [Credits](/contribute/credits) for all roles.

    Existing artists appear first. If the person is not listed, create an artist record from the editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and submit">
    Check the possible duplicates and the live preview. Agree to the truthfulness pledge, then submit.

    The song is **Under Review** until it is published. You go to the review area on your profile. There you can open and edit your pending song.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After review

Once published, the song appears in the catalogue and on the credited artists' pages. To correct it later, use **Suggest an edit** on the song page. Your changes are credited to you and shown in the song's activity.

<Note>
  Songs with Arabic script, Jawi, or other non-Latin titles get a stable URL slug for sharing.
</Note>

<Tip>
  You do not need every optional field. Submit the core facts you can verify. Add more later when you find reliable sources.
</Tip>

For the detailed credit workflow, see [Add and Edit Song Credits](/contribute/credits).
