People who care about Malaysian music build Diskograf. You can add a missing song, claim your credit, or suggest a correction.

## What you can contribute

  <Card title="Add a song" icon="music" href="/contribute/adding-songs">

## Getting access

You need a free account to contribute. Sign in with Google, finish onboarding, and choose a username and display name.

Any registered user can add a song or suggest an edit. Artists must link or create an artist profile before they can claim credits.

New songs stay private during review. Edits to existing songs are credited to your account and shown in the song's history.

[Create your Diskograf account](/accounts) to get started.

## Community standards

Diskograf depends on accurate contributions. Follow these rules:

- **Add only facts you can verify.** Use a physical release, a streaming platform, official liner notes, or a credible published source.
- **Claim only your own identity and work.** Never link to an artist profile that is not you.
- **Fix errors you find.** Use **Suggest an edit** for wrong credits, misspelled names, or wrong years.
- **If unsure, leave it blank.** An empty field is better than a guess. Anyone can fill it in once confirmed.

<Tip>
  Start with a credit you know is wrong or missing. Check a reliable source and the role. Submit only what you can support.
</Tip>
