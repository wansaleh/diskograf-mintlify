Diskograf is built entirely by the community — fans who grew up with these songs, musicians who made them, and collectors who have spent years tracking down every pressing and liner note. Every entry in the catalogue exists because someone cared enough to put it there. If you know something about Malaysian music that isn't in Diskograf yet, you are exactly the right person to add it.

## Getting Access

You need a free Diskograf account to contribute. Creating an account takes under a minute and gives you full access to add songs, edit credits, and update artist profiles right away.

[Create your Diskograf account](/accounts) to get started.

If you already have an account, sign in and you can begin contributing immediately — no approval process, no waiting period.

## Community Standards

Diskograf works because contributors hold themselves to a shared standard of accuracy. When you contribute, keep the following in mind:

- **Only add factual, verifiable information.** Every credit and every detail should be something you can point to — a physical release, a streaming platform, an official liner note, or a credible published source.
- **Do not claim artist profiles that aren't yours.** Falsely associating yourself with another artist's discography undermines trust for everyone. If you are the artist, see the artist identity and profile-claiming process.
- **Fix errors you find.** If you notice a wrong credit, a misspelled name, or an incorrect release year, go ahead and correct it. Every fix improves the archive for every future visitor.
- **When in doubt about a credit, leave it blank.** An empty field is more honest than a guess. You — or someone else — can always come back and fill it in once it's confirmed.

<Tip>
  Start by fixing a credit you know is wrong — it's the fastest way to make a difference. You already have the knowledge; Diskograf just needs you to put it on the record.
</Tip>
