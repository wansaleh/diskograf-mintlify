Diskograf should work for everyone who reads or contributes. Accessibility is part of what makes the archive useful.

## Our Commitment

Diskograf targets **WCAG 2.1 Level AA** across the site. In practice:

- **Text contrast**: body text is 4.5:1 or better. Large text is 3:1 or better.
- **Keyboard navigation** works on every page. You can browse, search, and edit without a mouse.
- **No color-only signals.** Errors and required fields pair color with an icon or label.
- **Reduced motion** is respected. With `prefers-reduced-motion`, you get a readable alternative, and no content is hidden.

## Contributor Tools

Contributor tools follow the same rules as the rest of the site. They work with a keyboard and screen readers. Validation never relies on color alone.

## Landing and Marketing Surfaces

Landing and campaign pages may use richer motion and expressive type. They still follow these rules:

- The full message and main actions work in a static, no-motion version.
- Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion` with a designed alternative.
- Keyboard access, visible focus, contrast, and touch target sizes stay intact.

Motion shows relationships in the archive. It is not decoration.

## Known Limitations

We track accessibility issues like any other bug. If something blocks you, tell us so we can fix it.

## Reporting an Accessibility Issue

If you hit an accessibility barrier, email:

```text
diskografcom@gmail.com
```

Include:

- The page or feature where you ran into the issue.
- What you were trying to do.
- Your assistive technology or setup, if you want to share it.

We reply to every report. Issues that block search, browsing, or reading a record come before cosmetic ones.
