A small team and a growing community run Diskograf. Start here to reach us.

## General Support

For help with the site or your account, email:

```text
diskografcom@gmail.com
```

Describe what you tried to do and what happened. Add screenshots for visual problems.

## Artist Identity and Claims

Use the same address for artist linking and impersonation reports. First read:

- [Claiming a Profile](/identity/claiming-profile)
- [Identity rules](/identity/verification)
- [Impersonation](/identity/impersonation)

Still need help? Email `diskografcom@gmail.com` with your username, the artist profile, and any supporting details.

## Data Corrections

Contributors make most corrections directly. Artists, labels, and rights holders can email `diskografcom@gmail.com` when a fix needs staff help. Include:

- A link to the record.
- What is currently wrong.
- The correct information, with a source if possible.

## Data Licensing and API Enquiries

Email `diskografcom@gmail.com` about the [Public API](/developers/public-api), higher rate limits, or larger exports. Also use it for licensing beyond the [Data Licensing](/developers/data-licensing) guidelines. Briefly describe what you are building.

## Press and Editorial

For press, editorial partnerships, or interviews, email `diskografcom@gmail.com` with **Press** in the subject line.

## Response Times

We read every message. Replies usually take a few business days. Include the page URL so we can find your issue.

<Note>
  Diskograf has no phone line or live chat. Email keeps a written record, which matters for identity and correction requests.
</Note>
